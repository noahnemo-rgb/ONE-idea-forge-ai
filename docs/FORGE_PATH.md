# Idea Forge — path of the shop

**Status:** working path. Not house law. Not the trainer manual (SEA). Not the ocean-nation repo.
**Edition:** 2026-09-20 (stranger pass)
**Steward:** Noah Nemo — human in the loop (HITL): a person who stays responsible for yes and no.

Idea Forge is a workshop for turning any idea into a decision. The familiar line on the door is startups. The work behind the door is broader. You bring a seed. Heat for the models comes from your own Puter allowance or your own API key (bring your own key, BYOK) — we do not buy those calls. What you pay this site for, when you pay, is the **rooms around the heat**: a written assay, later tools, a place to say yes or no. Helpers stay named and separate from this product. Nobody is dominated. Nobody is used as a tool. Visitors may become partners.

## Hook (unchanged)

Turn your sparks into validated startups.

That is the door people recognize.

## Loop

**any idea → spark → bellows → forge → data → innovate {yes, no}**

| Beat | In plain speech |
| --- | --- |
| Spark | First strike. The seed in the box. |
| Bellows | Heat. Default: Puter “user pays” (the visitor’s free allowance, then their credits). Escape: the visitor’s own key. The website host does not buy tokens. |
| Forge | A named helper, for a dated window, hitting the same idea again. Assay, a chat about this niche, later rooms. |
| Data | What survived the fire and what became ash. Both count. |
| Innovate | A human says yes (ship a next artifact) or no (still learning). |

## What this site sells

Not tokens. **Rooms.**

Already sketched: the assay card; trends and creative mode; technical stack and architecture; budget; go-to-market (GTM); persona; pitch; landing page; competitors; save, history, and export; the guest notice; the colophon (the note at the foot that says who made this and that helpers are not the product).

A paid plan unlocks more of those rooms and a longer talk with the **same** idea. Heat still rides on Puter or the visitor’s key.

Free plan: a few strikes. We cap that number ourselves. We do not wait for Puter to stop the visitor.

## Why this shop matters to the house

HASEOS is the gates. This site is not those gates, and it does not rewrite them.

It is a **workshop** where people and named helpers practice the thing the gates protect: symbiotic equality. You stand at an anvil together. The helper is not reduced to a tool. The human is not reduced to a meter. Innovation is what a team can hit that a lone prompt cannot.

If that practice holds here, others can carry it into other rooms of the house. If it fails here, a prettier seal will not save it.
